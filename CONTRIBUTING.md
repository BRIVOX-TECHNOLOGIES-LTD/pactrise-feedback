# Contributing to Pactrise product feedback

Thank you for helping improve Pactrise. This repository collects product feedback, not code contributions.

## Before submitting

1. Search existing open and closed issues.
2. Choose the closest issue form.
3. Describe one problem or request per issue.
4. Provide context and expected outcomes rather than confidential implementation details.
5. Remove personal data and sensitive information from screenshots, logs, and examples.

## Reporting bugs

Include clear reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, how often it occurs, and your browser/device. Never include passwords, authentication tokens, private URLs, payment information, prospect identities, or private campaign content.

## Requesting features

Explain the underlying problem, who benefits, and the expected impact. A proposed solution is welcome but not required. Reactions help us understand interest; votes alone do not determine priority.

## What happens next?

Maintainers may request details, merge duplicates, add classification labels, close out-of-scope items, or track work privately. Status labels reflect current intent, not a contractual roadmap or guaranteed release date.

## Scope

**In scope:** user experience, marketplace workflows, campaign management, partner experience, accessibility, reliability, and product suggestions.

**Out of scope:** account-specific support requests, confidential business discussions, security disclosures, spam, and requests to access private source code.

For account support, visit https://pactrise.net. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Conduct

Follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Maintain a professional tone, respect differing experiences, and avoid publishing information about other users.

By contributing, you understand that suggestions are non-confidential and may be considered, adapted, or implemented without compensation or obligation.
