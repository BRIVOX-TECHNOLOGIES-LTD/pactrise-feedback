## Welcome

This is the official public feedback repository for **Pactrise**, a B2B sales marketplace connecting companies with independent sales professionals through outcome-based campaigns.

We use this space to listen, investigate issues, discuss improvements, and communicate product progress. The Pactrise application source code and internal engineering work remain private.

## Our product principles

**Outcome-oriented.** We focus on real business outcomes: qualified attended meetings and verified completed sales.

**Trust by design.** Privacy, attribution, clear terms, and fair verification are fundamental to the marketplace.

**Practical improvements.** We prioritize feedback that improves clarity, reliability, and the ability of companies and sales partners to succeed.

**Transparent communication.** We aim to acknowledge actionable feedback and explain decisions when practical. An accepted suggestion is not a delivery commitment.

## How feedback moves forward

`Submitted → Triage → Under Review → Planned → In Progress → Released`

Some requests may be marked `Needs Info`, `Duplicate`, or `Not Planned`. These are working categories, not promises or fixed timelines. Public feedback may be converted into private engineering tasks.

Please search for similar requests before opening a new issue. Add a reaction or a constructive comment to an existing request rather than opening a duplicate.

## What makes useful feedback?

- **Context:** Are you a company, a sales partner, or a prospective user?
- **Problem:** What are you trying to accomplish, and what is getting in the way?
- **Impact:** How often does this happen, and what does it prevent you from doing?
- **Expected outcome:** What would a successful experience look like?

For bugs, include reproducible steps, expected versus actual behavior, device/browser details, and sanitized screenshots when helpful.
