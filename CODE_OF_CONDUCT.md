# Community code of conduct

Pactrise is committed to a constructive, professional, and inclusive feedback community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate of other participants.
- Focus on observable problems, practical solutions, and useful context.
- Assume good faith while allowing for disagreement.
- Protect the privacy and confidentiality of customers, prospects, and sales partners.
- Keep discussions relevant to the product.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, spam, impersonation, publication of private information, and attempts to disclose or exploit vulnerabilities in public discussions are not permitted.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit labels, lock conversations, hide inappropriate content, or close issues to protect the community and keep discussion productive. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to GitHub.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact Pactrise through https://pactrise.net and request a confidential escalation.
